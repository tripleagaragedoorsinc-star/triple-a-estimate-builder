# Pricing comparison specification

Sources:
1. Public calculator: indicative range only. Its 27% minimum reduction must not be applied to internal selling prices.
2. Supplier invoices: verified unit costs, with 10% cost escalation and 2x material selling multiplier.
3. ServiceTitan: distinguish proposed, sold and completed amounts. Imported default items require manual classification.

Pricing:
- Material target = supplier unit cost * 1.10 * 2.
- Labor = 550 to 1000 per installed door, selected by complexity.
- Disposal = 150 for one door, 200 for two doors; larger jobs require owner approval.
- Add separately verified accessories, delivery and custom charges.
- Do not treat a calculated target as an observed sales average.

Initial verified examples:
- Amarr Lincoln LI3000 8x7: supplier 689.74; material target 1517.43.
- CHI 3282 10x12: supplier 1750.00; material target 3850.00.
- LiftMaster RO LM 2220L: supplier 286.00; material target 629.20.

All supplier prices belong in owner-only storage. Do not expose them to sales staff or customer exports.
